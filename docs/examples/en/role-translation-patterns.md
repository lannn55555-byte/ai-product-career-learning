# Example: Role Translation Patterns

These are short, anonymized patterns for understanding the toolkit. They are not hiring guarantees.

| Background | Possible direction | Lead-now proof | Translate next | First gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2B product manager | AI application product | approval states, permissions, exceptions | domain entities and SOPs → retrieval boundaries and tool inputs | evaluation and trade-offs |
| Product or UX designer | AI UX design | journeys, usability tests, interaction hierarchy | error states and editable outputs → uncertainty and recovery design | model limits and evaluation |
| Operations lead | AI application product | SOP optimization, exceptions, handoffs | operational rules → human-in-the-loop workflow | product metrics and tools |
| Learning designer | AI learning product | outcomes, feedback design, learner observation | rubrics and misconceptions → evaluation criteria | discovery and instrumentation |
