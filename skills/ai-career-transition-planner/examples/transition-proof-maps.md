# Six AI Career Proof Maps / 六类转 AI 职业证据地图

These are short, anonymized examples for understanding the output. They are not claims about real users or hiring outcomes.

以下是帮助理解输出的匿名简例，不代表真实用户或求职结果。

| Background / 背景 | Primary direction / 主攻方向 | Lead-now proof / 可立即主打 | Translate-next asset / 优先转化 | First gap / 第一短板 | Next Skill / 下一步 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS PM | AI Agent Product | Approval states, permissions, exceptions | CRM data model and SOP → tool inputs, retrieval boundary | Evaluation and quality/latency/cost trade-offs | Sprint Plan |
| Product/UX Designer | AI UX Designer | User flows, usability testing, interaction hierarchy | Error states and editable output → AI uncertainty/recovery design | LLM limits and AI evaluation | Sprint Plan |
| Operations Lead | AI Application Product | SOP optimization, exceptions, handoffs | Operational rules and source-of-truth data → human-in-the-loop workflow | Product metrics and tool-calling concepts | Case Strategy |
| Growth/Content Marketer | AI Content Product | Audience insight, content experiments, funnel thinking | Content taxonomy and editorial workflow → retrieval, generation, review loop | Reliability, rights, and cost controls | Sprint Plan |
| Educator/Learning Designer | AI Learning Product | Learning outcomes, feedback design, learner observation | Rubrics and misconception patterns → evaluation criteria and guardrails | Product discovery and data instrumentation | Case Strategy |
| Cross-border E-commerce Operator | AI Commerce Product | Catalog operations, localization, customer objections | Product attributes, policy rules, and regional SOP → controlled generation/tool workflow | User research and AI UX recovery | Sprint Plan |
