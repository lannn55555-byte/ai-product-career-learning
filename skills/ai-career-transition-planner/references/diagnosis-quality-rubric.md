# Diagnosis Quality Rubric / 诊断质量标准

Apply this checklist before returning a core diagnosis.

## Must pass / 必须满足

- Every stated strength names a real experience, artifact, or user-provided fact.
- Every claimed personal contribution is distinct from team contribution.
- Every role score identifies its evidence source and confidence level.
- Without a supplied JD, the diagnosis uses Evidence Readiness `/60`, not a fabricated Path Fit `/100`.
- Without a stated time constraint, the diagnosis does not score short-sprint closability.
- Domain assets name concrete data structures, SOP, user knowledge, or workflow knowledge; do not label them RAG-ready without evidence.
- Gaps are actionable and ranked. Do not list more than three priority gaps.
- The response ends with exactly one recommended next Skill, not a full unsolicited plan.

## Fail conditions / 失败条件

- Generic praise that could apply to any candidate.
- A numerical score without a visible breakdown or explanation.
- Claims of AI deployment, business impact, ownership, or metrics that the user did not provide.
- Treating prompt usage as proof of AI product expertise.
- Recommending a complex demo, RAG system, or multi-agent build without a role-specific reason.
